The downfall of Marvel Rivals

Marvel Rivals took the world by storm.... Reaching tremendous player counts, yet today... following controversy after controversy they are a shell of the game they once were.

Production, Community, and Insensitivity

Over the past 3 Seasons in Marvel Rivals there have been constant controversy, whether it be the Skin for one of the characters not be legendary because of her skin tone, or the removal of racism from the options to report a player, Rivals has not made it any less obvious of their intentions.
